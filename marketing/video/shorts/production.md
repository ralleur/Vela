## Additional distinct follow-ups

- **One retry, another engine**: show a known failing local fixture and the real Try Compatible Playback action. Explain VLC/mpv without promising universal decoding. Capture the error and successful retry before publishing.
- **No account for a file**: cold account-free startup, native Open Video, playback. Demonstrate the absence of a signup flow without claiming zero network traffic.
- **A place beside your work**: enter and exit the floating mini player. Call it a mini player; do not label it system Picture-in-Picture.
