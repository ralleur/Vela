# Vela: 20 English Shorts built around a reason to keep watching

Status: local campaign production. No uploads, audience results or reach guarantees. Created 3 October 2026.

## The decision

Lead with **a free Mac player that handles local files and Jellyfin in one place**. Make people recognise a small irritation, then show its resolution. The existing promise stays: **Just watch the video.** A source connection, a disappearing control or a visible subtitle option is the proof, rather than a paragraph about the app.

Hauser is the editing reference: immediate large type, real UI, close framing, visual reveals, restrained musical hits. Vela keeps its own navy/blue palette, mark and Mac identity. No smart-home content, iPhone mockups or TestFlight CTA.

## Who each video is for

1. Mac users looking for useful free desktop software: free whole app, MKV opening, no account for a local file.
2. Jellyfin users who also watch files on their Mac: their library and local files reach the same player. Jellyfin remains a separate server, not a service bundled with Vela.
3. People who value quiet software: the controls yield to the film; language choices are accessible.
4. Open-source and indie-app viewers: Swiftfin lineage and the founder's reason for adding local playback. These support trust and affinity; they are not assumed to be the widest topics.

Every opening visibly says FOR MAC. A mobile viewer should understand that this is a Mac app without being misled by the vertical format.

## What YouTube actually tells us

YouTube describes personalised recommendations informed by choosing to watch, average view duration, average percentage viewed and satisfaction signals. It does not promise a favourite editing format or require a minimum publishing cadence. Topic demand and competition also matter. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225?hl=en

Public Shorts views count starts/replays without a minimum watch duration. Evaluate **engaged views**, **stayed to watch**, retention and watch time together; raw view starts alone are not evidence that a hook worked. Average duration/percentage viewed use engaged viewers, so strong retention can coexist with many immediate swipes. Sources: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12220281?hl=en and https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/333869549?hl=en

These are platform facts. The durations, cutting patterns, first-three-second brief and release order below are our creative hypotheses, not published algorithm thresholds. Shares and useful comments are audience-response diagnostics, not a claimed hidden ranking formula.

## Editing rules for this series

- Frame 0 contains the hook and recognisable evidence; no logo animation or blank lead-in. Usually 4–8 hook words, split into two readable lines.
- By 0.8–1.4 seconds something resolves or changes: controls/clean comparison, local/library pairing, a free-price reveal or a focus on the relevant menu item. By 3 seconds the viewer understands what is unusual.
- The body earns the hook: one argument and at most three short steps. Each cut reveals new information, usually every 1.5–3 seconds. Reading time wins over arbitrary rapid cuts.
- Show the benefit before the download prompt. Vela's name appears after the opening has started. Finish over real playback, with a short Vela/free-Mac-beta overlay and the project address. No four-second static ad card or large QR on the same phone being used to watch.
- English burned-in text and separate captions; understandable muted. Existing original Hauser music provides the family resemblance; film soundtrack is muted.
- Move and crop real captured UI; never fabricate taps, menus, failures, latency tests or competitor screens. The controls/clean wipe is an editorial comparison of matching captured states, not a timed interaction recording.
- Give readable screen area to the proof. A whole landscape desktop shrunk under a huge title is insufficient. Menu/detail crops retain their real geometry; playback shots retain a clear player context.

## The 20 concepts and their test structure

The numbered JSON is the editable production source. Each item includes audience, reason to stay, alternate hook, exact beat timing, proof and a current description. The different price/source/UI entries are deliberate creative variants across a few true reasons to use Vela; they do not pretend to be twenty unrelated product features.

Start with **V01, V02, V03, V04 and V07**: free discovery, Jellyfin crossover, visual satisfaction, MKV utility and subtitles. These probe distinct interests and forms. Release one at a time; a suggested working cadence is three to four per week, adjusted to your ability to read results and respond. This is an editorial choice, not an algorithm requirement.

Do not schedule the remaining fifteen blindly. After the first five have comparable observation windows and useful exposure, select the next group from the strongest promise:

- Free/value → V09 and V16, with different openings and examples.
- Local + Jellyfin → V05, V08, V10, V18, V19 and V20.
- Quiet UI → V12 and V14.
- Practical utility → V06, V15 and V17.
- Trust and project identity → V11 and V13, interleaved when the audience knows what Vela is.

Producing all 20 now makes the next options ready. It does not make every variant equally worth publishing.

## Measure, then change one major thing

No channel Analytics was supplied or accessed. There is no channel-specific baseline and no evidence for a guaranteed best time, retention percentage or view count.

Log results after comparable 48-hour and 7-day windows. Treat low-exposure posts as inconclusive and allow delayed distribution; do not delete or mass re-upload after one slow day. Compare like-length videos and note traffic-source differences. Organic comparisons are directional, not randomised A/B tests.

- Lots of immediate swipes: inspect the first frame, audience qualification and hook wording. Test the alternate hook on a new cut.
- People enter but leave before proof: move the proof earlier; remove setup and extra reading.
- The reveal loses viewers: the payoff may not fulfil the promise. Fix the story instead of withholding the answer longer.
- Views and viewing time without useful interest: strengthen the Mac/Jellyfin fit and show Vela's name clearly.
- Engaged viewing plus interest in downloading: clarify the route to the download. Avoid claiming that anonymous downloads can be assigned to an individual Short without attribution.

Use the supplied results CSV for feed exposure where available, stayed-to-watch percentage, engaged views, average duration/percentage, subscribers gained and useful comments. Supplement with profile/website/referral data only if actually available. Pick winners on audience fit and viewing together, rather than a universal score.
