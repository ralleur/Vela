Privacy in Vela.

Last updated: 4 October 2026. Vela is an independent, free, open-source player by Ralleur. Mac is available in beta; iPhone/iPad and Apple TV releases are in preparation.

Your local videos

Local playback on Mac and the iPhone/iPad development version does not require a Vela or Jellyfin account. Vela reads files you choose and does not upload those video files to Ralleur. Local playback does not create Jellyfin playback reports.

History and settings

Recent-file references, playback positions, track choices and settings are stored on your device. You can clear or disable local history in Settings. This removes saved history, not your video files. Device backups may include app data according to your operating-system settings.

Your Jellyfin server

When you connect Jellyfin, Vela sends sign-in requests, media requests and server-playback progress to the server you configure. Access tokens are stored using Apple's Keychain. The server operator controls its own logs and data. A connected server session can continue normal network activity while you watch a local file.

Episode information

For supported series outlooks, Vela may contact TVmaze for episode or season information. This is associated with Jellyfin series features, not local-file playback.

Diagnostics and permissions

Vela has no advertising or integrated analytics service. It keeps local diagnostic logs for troubleshooting. If you share logs in a support request, check them for server addresses and other private information first. File and local-network permissions are used for the features you choose. Apple may process system diagnostics according to your device settings.

Contact and source

For privacy questions, use the Vela issue tracker; do not post passwords, access tokens or private media there. You can inspect the source code and release preparation notes.
