# Vela 0.9.4 Beta

Your files. Your Jellyfin library. One player.

[Download Vela for Mac](https://github.com/ralleur/Vela/releases/download/vela-0.9.4/Vela-0.9.4-macOS-universal.dmg) · [Project website](https://ralleur.github.io/Vela/)

## Install

Requires **macOS 15.6 or later**, on Apple silicon or Intel. Open the DMG and
drag Vela into Applications. Quit an older Vela before replacing it.
The app is **Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple**, with a stapled ticket.
This installer replaces the development-signed ZIP used for previous betas.

Version **0.9.4**, build **5**. DMG size: **140,288,000 bytes** (133.8 MiB).

## In this release

- Open local videos through Finder, Open Video or drag and drop; no Jellyfin
  account is required for local files.
- Browse and play your Jellyfin library through the Swiftfin-derived client.
- Use one player for both sources, with audio/subtitle selection, seeking,
  playback speed and fullscreen controls.
- Resume local files using sandbox-aware recent-file access. Manage saved
  positions and track choices in Settings.
- Choose VLC or mpv for local playback, with a compatible-playback retry.
- Mac menus expose file opening, playback options, Settings and accounts.
  Player navigation chrome stays hidden when moving between sources.

Vela is free and open source, with no subscription, in-app purchase or Pro tier.
It is an independent fork of [Swiftfin](https://github.com/jellyfin/Swiftfin),
not an official or endorsed Jellyfin/Swiftfin app.

## Validation and beta limits

The universal Release build passed bundle, architecture, sandbox and strict
code-signature checks. Apple notarization and Gatekeeper assessment passed.
The app was launched from this exact DMG and local MKV playback was observed.
The launch qualification also passed 44 logic tests and exercised local/Jellyfin
playback, track menus, seeking, pause/resume and fullscreen on Apple silicon.

The Intel slice is included but has not been runtime-tested on an Intel Mac.
The oldest supported macOS version, long playback sessions and every codec/HDR
combination have not been exhaustively qualified. This remains a beta.
This release offers the Mac installer; it does not announce a new tvOS or iOS
binary release.
